- Object.freeze on a Map does not stop Map.prototype.set
  trigger: when freezing a ReadonlyMap returned from a public API
  kind: gotcha
  cause: freeze locks object properties, not a Map's internal entries
  resolution: freeze the map, its arrays and its records, and say in TSDoc that only a write casting away ReadonlyMap can change the result
  artifact: Object.freeze(copy)
  signal: silent
